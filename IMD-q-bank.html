Sources & References

Questions reflect standard foundational concepts in pharmacology and pathophysiology common across major pre-med and medical curricula, rather than being drawn verbatim from any single text. Core reference material includes:

  • Katzung's Basic & Clinical Pharmacology
  • Goodman & Gilman's Pharmacological Basis of Therapeutics
  • Lippincott Illustrated Reviews: Pharmacology
  • StatPearls / NCBI Bookshelf
  • Merck Manual (Professional & Consumer editions)
  • Mayo Clinic Patient Care & Health Information
  • MedlinePlus (U.S. National Library of Medicine)
  • Harvard Health Publishing
  • World Health Organization (WHO) clinical guidance
  • OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology
  • Robbins Basic Pathology
  • U.S. FDA drug safety communications

This tool is for educational, self-assessment purposes only and is not a substitute for a licensed medical curriculum, clinical training, or professional medical advice.
